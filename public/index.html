Document Check Does what someone typed match the document they gave you?
  1. Pick the document. Twelve Indian documents are supported, from an Aadhaar card to a GST certificate.
  2. Add a photo, a scan or a PDF of it. Nothing is stored — the picture is used once and thrown away.
  3. Let it fill the form in, or type the details yourself if you already have them.
  4. Press check. You get a plain answer, field by field, with what the document actually says.

Every sample document here is made up. This is a demonstration — it does not contact any government database, and it cannot tell a good forgery from a real document. Never put a real ID through it.
