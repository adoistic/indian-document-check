Document Check Does what someone typed match the document they gave you?
  1. Pick the document. Fifteen Indian documents are supported, from an Aadhaar card to a certificate of incorporation.
  2. Add a photo, a scan or a PDF of it. Nothing is stored — the picture is used once and thrown away.
  3. Let it fill the form in, or type the details yourself if you already have them.
  4. Press check. You get a plain answer, field by field, with what the document actually says.
  1. Drop in everything at once. Any mix of documents, for any number of people. You do not have to say what they are.
  2. Each one is worked out on its own — what kind of document it is, and every name, date and reference number on it.
  3. Then they are sorted into who and what they belong to, by comparing those numbers and names against each other.
  4. Connections are drawn out — which person runs which business, who is a director of what. Every grouping tells you why.

Every sample document here is made up. This is a demonstration — it does not contact any government database, and it cannot tell a good forgery from a real document. Never put a real ID through it.
